Privacy

Short version: nothing leaves your browser

  • The game saves your progress, language and radio station in your browser's local storage on your own device. We cannot see it. Clear your site data and it is gone.
  • No accounts, no cookies, no analytics, no ads.
  • If the site owner has connected a form inbox, the vote and bug-report forms send only what you type. Otherwise they store your answers on your device only.
  • The pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so Google sees a normal font request from your browser.

A fan-made game. Every shop and character is fictional.
