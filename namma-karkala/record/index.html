Record the voices for Namma Karkala

How to record (1 minute)
  • Use a quiet room. Hold the phone or laptop about a hand's length from your mouth.
  • Press Record, wait a moment, say the line like the character would say it, then press Stop. Pauses at commas and full stops are good: that is what makes it sound human.
  • You can say it in your own natural words. If you change the words, tap Edit the words and type what you said, so the subtitles match your voice.
  • Different people can record different characters. Use the Character menu so each person does their own.
  • Your recordings stay on this device (the page keeps them even if you close it). Press Download my recordings when you want to send them: you get one file (a zip). Send it to me in the chat, or upload it to the voice/incoming folder on GitHub.
