How to play

Controls and tips

Namma Karkala is built for a laptop or desktop with a keyboard. Chrome, Edge, Safari and Firefox all work.

Controls

  • W A S D walk or drive, Shift run, mouse to look around
  • F get on or off any vehicle, talk to people marked with a yellow "!", order at a tindi counter
  • H horn · Space jump or handbrake · C camera distance
  • R change radio station (in a bus, auto, car, tempo or lorry)
  • J side jobs · M big map · O photo mode (Enter saves a picture) · Esc pause

Things to find

  • Pili Vesha at Swaraj Maidan, afternoon and evening. Yakshagana at Gandhi Maidan, evening and night. Bhajane near the Hanuman Temple, early morning and evening. Kambala in the fields beside Aanekere, daytime.
  • Tindi counters at the tiffin hotels, lunch homes and street carts. Press F, then a number.
  • Neighbours chatting in bubbles. Walk up to them.
  • The road shouts back if you drive in the middle, nearly hit someone or spam the horn.

Cheat codes

Type the word while playing: BARSA rain · SURYA clear sky · IRULU night · BELAKU morning · PAISA money · POLICEILLA clear wanted level · GOLIBAJE full health · BAHUBALI moon jump · JORR super sprint · BUSBEKU AUTOBEKU BULLET LORRY TRACTOR AMBULANCE CYCLE KSRTC spawn that vehicle.

Will it run on my laptop?

Most laptops from the last several years, yes. The game lowers its own detail if the frame rate drops. A laptop with a graphics chip will look best. The first visit downloads the town (about 20 MB), after that it opens in seconds. If it feels slow, close other tabs, plug the laptop in, and in the pause menu turn the music off.

Time and weather

By default the game follows the real clock and sky of Karkala (India time): morning in the game when it is morning there, dark when it is dark, rain when it rains. The shows keep fixed hours, so the pause menu has Time: Karkala live / game clock. On the game clock a day takes two real hours. Cheat words: SANJE evening, IRULU night, BELAKU morning, LIVE back to real time. If the live weather cannot be reached (offline), the game uses its own weather.

Language

The title screen and pause menu let you switch dialogue between ಕನ್ನಡ, ತುಳು and English. Voices use real recordings when they are installed, otherwise your browser's speech voice if it has one, otherwise text.
